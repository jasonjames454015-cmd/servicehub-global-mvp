## Q2 2026 figures read from the official PDF
On 2026-10-01 the catalog ZIP (1,126,960 bytes) was downloaded and the file `Q2_2026_GDP_Report_.pdf` (catalog date 2026-08-31) was text-extracted. The full PDF is not stored in this repo. Figures below are from that extract, not from secondary news.

From the overview:
- Real GDP grew 4.43% year-on-year in Q2 2026, versus 4.23% in Q2 2025.
- Agriculture real growth 4.39% (versus 2.82% in Q2 2025).
- Industry real growth 3.96% (versus 7.46% in Q2 2025).
- Services real growth 4.60% (versus 3.94% in Q2 2025); services share of aggregate GDP 56.62% (versus 56.53% a year earlier).
- Nominal aggregate GDP at basic prices N119,294,681.84 million, versus N100,730,501.10 million in Q2 2025 (nominal growth 18.43%).

Construction (report section):
- Nominal growth 27.38% year-on-year (versus 9.58% in Q2 2025); quarter-on-quarter nominal growth -14.47%.
- Nominal GDP share 4.29% (versus 3.99% a year earlier and 5.41% in Q1 2026).
- Real growth 6.75% year-on-year, 1.48 percentage points above the prior-year quarter.
- Real GDP share in the accompanying table: 3.68% in Q2 2026 (Q1 2026 was 4.85%).

Real Estate Services (report section):
- Nominal growth 13.85% year-on-year, 7.71 percentage points above Q2 2025; quarter-on-quarter nominal growth -44.61%.
- Nominal GDP share 8.71% (versus 9.06% in Q2 2025 and 16.94% in Q1 2026).
- Real growth 3.76%, 0.03 percentage points below Q2 2025 and 1.47 percentage points above Q1 2026.
- Quarter-on-quarter real growth 1.26%.
- Real GDP share 12.71% (versus 12.80% in Q2 2025 and 13.10% in Q1 2026).

These are national accounts, not Lagos transaction prices and not a valuation.

## Housing stock / deficit (attributed, not independently audited here)
Federal Ministry of Housing and Urban Development / National Housing Data Technical Committee figures repeated in 2026 briefings and news:
- Official national housing deficit cited at about 14.92–14.925 million units.
- Additional ~15.2 million units described as structurally deficient/substandard in the same committee narrative (this is **not** the same as the official deficit).
- Lagos State Commissioner for Housing (Maroof Akinderu-Fatai), 2026 ministerial briefing: 10,623 units delivered in seven years under the current administration; 4,414 (or 4,440 in some transcripts) via direct budget and 6,209 via PPP; target stock ~14,222 units if named schemes complete in 2026.
- Lagos deficit independently estimated in later 2026 official-adjacent remarks at about 3.3–3.4 million units; annual need cited around 227,576 units; capital need cited around N6 trillion/year. These are political/briefing numbers, not a survey you can sell as a census.

Do **not** merge committee deficit, substandard stock, and Lagos delivery counts into one “market size.”
