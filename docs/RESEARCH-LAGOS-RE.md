## Housing stock / deficit (attributed, not independently audited here)
Federal Ministry of Housing and Urban Development / National Housing Data Technical Committee figures repeated in 2026 briefings and news:
- Official national housing deficit cited at about 14.92–14.925 million units.
- Additional ~15.2 million units described as structurally deficient/substandard in the same committee narrative (this is **not** the same as the official deficit).
- Lagos State Commissioner for Housing (Maroof Akinderu-Fatai), 2026 ministerial briefing: 10,623 units delivered in seven years under the current administration; 4,414 (or 4,440 in some transcripts) via direct budget and 6,209 via PPP; target stock ~14,222 units if named schemes complete in 2026.
- Lagos deficit independently estimated in later 2026 official-adjacent remarks at about 3.3–3.4 million units; annual need cited around 227,576 units; capital need cited around N6 trillion/year. These are political/briefing numbers, not a survey you can sell as a census.

Do **not** merge committee deficit, substandard stock, and Lagos delivery counts into one “market size.”
