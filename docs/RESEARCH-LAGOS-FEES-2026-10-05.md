# Lagos agency and agreement fee screen

Read: 2026-10-05. Public government pages only. Not legal advice, not brokerage, not a fee quote for a named transaction.

## What the official pages say

1. Lagos State news, 13 May 2025, “LASG Reaffirms 10% Cap on Tenancy Agreement Fees”  
   https://lagosstate.gov.ng/news/all/view/6825b9ac5e4c9d6ceca455b8  
   Special Adviser on Housing, Barr. Barakat Odunuga-Bakare, said the maximum tenancy agreement / legal fee remains 10% of annual rent. Demanding more was described as a breach of the Lagos State Tenancy Law. Residents were told to report excess demands via LASRERA (Facebook, Instagram lasrera 1, X @lasrera).

2. Lagos State news, 19 June 2025, “Lasrera Partners Cdas, Cdcs of 57 Lgas/lcdas to Reduce Agency, Legal Fees”  
   https://lagosstate.gov.ng/news/all/view/685588c05e4c9d6cecaa165a  
   Same office said agency fees must be between zero and 10 percent, and that anything beyond that is unacceptable. Public was told to verify agent registration on the LASRERA site before a transaction and to file complaints against defaulters. Commissioner for Housing, Hon. Maruf Akinderu Fatai, framed the LGA/LCDA partnership as a fee-reduction and order exercise, not a new price index.

## What this screen does not say

- A 2026 blog claim of a proposed 5% agency-fee bill is not treated as law here. No official gazette page for that bill was read on 2026-10-05.
- These pages are not a substitute for the Tenancy Law text or a LASRERA registration lookup on a named agent.
- National GDP and listing-site asking prices are not used in this screen.

## Desk use

Before any Lagos letting conversation: ask for the LASRERA registration reference, the annual rent, and the fee line items. Flag any agency or agreement line above 10% of annual rent as a question for a licensed practitioner, not as a finding this desk can enforce.

Paid briefing band only after a written yes: $80–$150. Sales recorded: $0.
