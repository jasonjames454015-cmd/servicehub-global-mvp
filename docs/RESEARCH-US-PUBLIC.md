# US real-estate public-source desk (compliance-first)

Date: 2026-09-25
Status: research pointers only. Not an MLS, not brokerage, not a CMA unless you hold the right license and data license.

## Free official / public sources

- FHFA HPI: https://www.fhfa.gov/data/hpi
- Census ACS / AHS: https://data.census.gov
- County assessor + recorder: search "[county] assessor"
- County / city GIS parcels when published
- data.gov parcel/cadastral search
- Zillow Research public downloads: https://www.zillow.com/research/data/ (read terms before commercial reuse)

## What these sources are not
Live MLS status, title plant, licensed appraisal, or permission to scrape consumer listing sites.

## Fair-housing note
Housing-related pages must be available without regard to protected class. Do not add steering language.
