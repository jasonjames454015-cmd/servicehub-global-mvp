## England / NI — Stamp Duty Land Tax (GOV.UK)

SDLT applies in **England and Northern Ireland** (not Scotland LBTT or Wales LTT).

Official pages:

- Residential rates: https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax/residential-property-rates
- Additional dwelling higher rates: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/stamp-duty-land-tax-buying-an-additional-residential-property
- First-time buyer relief manual excerpt: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/stamp-duty-land-tax-manual/sdltm29815

### Main-residence bands (from 1 April 2025)

| Slice | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Up to £125,000 | 0% |
| £125,001–£250,000 | 2% |
| £250,001–£925,000 | 5% |
| £925,001–£1,500,000 | 10% |
| Above £1,500,000 | 12% |

GOV.UK worked example: house bought April 2025 for **£295,000** → SDLT **£4,750**.

### First-time buyers (price ≤ £500,000)

- 0% on first £300,000
- 5% on £300,001–£500,000
- Over £500,000: no relief; use standard bands

### Additional dwellings (from 1 April 2025)

| Slice | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Up to £125,000 | 5% |
| £125,001–£250,000 | 7% |
| £250,001–£925,000 | 10% |
| £925,001–£1,500,000 | 15% |
| Above £1,500,000 | 17% |

GOV.UK example: additional home **£300,000** on 1 April 2025 → SDLT **£20,000**.

Non-UK residents: extra 2% surcharge still referenced on GOV.UK (confirm current page before any client memo).
