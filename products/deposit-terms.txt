SERVICEHUB GLOBAL — DEPOSIT LANGUAGE (DRAFT, NOT LEGAL ADVICE)
Use only after a written yes. Adapt for your jurisdiction. This is a worksheet, not a contract pack.

1. Deposit
A deposit of ___% of the quoted price is due before materials are ordered or a start date is locked.
The balance is due on completion of the agreed scope, unless staged payments are listed below.

2. Scope lock
Work is limited to the written scope dated ______. Extra work needs a written variation and a new price.

3. Materials
Quoted materials assume the list attached. Substitutions of equal specification may be used if the original item is unavailable; the client is told before the change.

4. Access and delays
The client provides safe access on agreed dates. Delay caused by missing access, unpaid deposit, or client-side changes is not the provider's delay.

5. Cancellation
If the client cancels after materials are ordered, the deposit covers committed materials and time already spent, up to the deposit amount. Unused remainder, if any, is discussed in writing.

6. Not included
Permits, licensed inspections, hidden defects found after opening up, and third-party fees are excluded unless named in the scope.

This text is a starting point. It is not a solicitor-drafted contract.
