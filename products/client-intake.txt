ServiceHub Global — Client intake (free template)
Not a contract. Not a paid engagement until both sides sign a scope.

1. Client name / business
2. WhatsApp or email (one primary channel only)
3. Problem in one sentence
4. Desired outcome in one sentence
5. Deadline (real date or “no date”)
6. Budget band they volunteered (leave blank if they did not)
7. Scope in / scope out
8. Deliverable format (doc, sheet, page, SOP)
9. Who must approve the work
10. Next step + date you will follow up

Rules
- Do not start unpaid “discovery” that eats a week.
- Do not invent a budget they did not state.
- Do not take deposits on platforms that are not live here.
- If they go silent, one follow-up on day 2 and one on day 7. Then stop.
