ServiceHub Global — 7-day follow-up SOP (free lead magnet)
Price if installed for a shop: $250–$750. This file itself is free.

Day 0 (same day as quote)
- Send quote PDF / photo.
- One line: price, what is included, what is not, valid-until date.

Day 1
- Did you get the quote? I can adjust scope if the budget is tighter.

Day 3
- Short case: similar job, days to finish, one photo if you have permission.

Day 5
- Offer two options only: full scope vs smaller first slice.

Day 7
- Close or park. Should I hold this slot or close the file?

Rules
- Stop if they say no.
- Do not invent urgency.
- Log every send in a sheet (date, channel, result).
- Never mark the job won without payment proof.
